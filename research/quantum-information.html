Research domain

Quantum Information

I am interested in the theoretical and computational structures that make quantum systems useful: resource theories, algorithms, non-classicality, and the relationship between abstract quantum advantage and physical implementation.

Selected work

  • Stabilizer entropies and non-stabilizerness
  • Quantum algorithms including QFT and Grover search
  • Quantum key distribution and CHSH-based security ideas

Questions I care about

  • What makes a quantum state or operation useful?
  • When do quantum resources become operational advantages?
  • How do theoretical metrics connect to real devices?

Methods

  • Quantum simulation
  • Python and QuTiP
  • Resource-theoretic analysis

Future directions

  • Quantum advantage under real hardware constraints
  • Fault-tolerant resource requirements
  • Links between information theory and experimental platforms
