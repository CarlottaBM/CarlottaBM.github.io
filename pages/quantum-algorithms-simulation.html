Project summary

I have used Python and QuTiP to implement and study quantum algorithms and simple quantum systems. These projects helped me connect abstract circuit descriptions to state evolution, measurement outcomes, and resource structure.

Rather than treating algorithms only as formal procedures, I am interested in what happens dynamically: which resources are generated, which structures are amplified, and how the computation changes when noise or physical constraints are introduced.

Topics to include

Grover search

Search, oracle structure, amplitude amplification, and resource evolution during computation.

Quantum Fourier transform

Circuit structure, phase information, and the role of the QFT in quantum algorithms.

Quantum walks

Quantum versus classical transport, graph dynamics, and applications to optimization and physical simulation.

Future directions

I want to use simulation as a bridge between quantum information theory and experimental quantum systems. In the future, I would like to explore how resource diagnostics, error models, and hardware constraints can be built into simulations of near-term quantum devices.
