Data-Driven Experimental Analysis

I work on analysis pipelines that turn imperfect measurements into physically meaningful evidence.

Much of this work is tied to research settings where raw data is not public. This page therefore keeps the description short and points to the CV/contact path for details.

Work areas

CR-39 Detector Image Analysis

Image-analysis workflows for track geometry, detector features, and physical inference from etched detector images.

Neutron and Background Measurements

Statistical analysis of detector backgrounds, calibration runs, count-rate distributions, and uncertainty in low-signal experimental settings.

Quantum Engineering Measurements

Experience connecting idealized quantum protocols to real measurement constraints, calibration, and noisy data.
