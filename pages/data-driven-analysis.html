Data-Driven Experimental Analysis

Many scientific questions depend not only on building an experiment, but on extracting reliable meaning from imperfect measurements. I am interested in data analysis workflows that connect detector signals, images, uncertainty, calibration, and physical interpretation.

This direction collects work on detector analysis, image processing, statistical characterization, and experimental validation. It is where my interests in physics, measurement, and machine learning meet.

Selected projects

CR-39 Detector Image Analysis

Developing image-analysis and machine-learning workflows to extract track geometry, classify detector features, and infer physical information from etched CR-39 detector images.

Neutron and Background Measurements

Statistical analysis of detector backgrounds, calibration runs, count-rate distributions, and uncertainty in low-signal experimental settings.

Quantum Engineering Measurements

Coursework and project experience with quantum measurement platforms, including the challenge of connecting idealized protocols to real experimental data.

Questions I want to pursue

  • How can we extract robust physical meaning from noisy or sparse experimental data?
  • How should measurement uncertainty and calibration be built into scientific workflows?
  • How can machine learning support experimental science without hiding the physics?
